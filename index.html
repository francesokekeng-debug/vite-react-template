Albrin Junior

Founder and managing director. Writer, director, producer.

Albrin Junior is a Nigerian author and filmmaker based in Glasgow. His collection of short stories, From Glasgow Without Love, is set between the two places and deals with identity, migration and displacement.

He adapted the book into a short film as writer, director and producer. His earlier short, Hadassah, screened at festivals in the UK and Nigeria.

“I felt this narrative could travel further through visual storytelling.”Albrin Junior, in The Nollywood Reporter
